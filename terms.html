1. Eligibility Requirements

All renters must meet the following criteria to be eligible for a PrestigeDrive vehicle rental:

  • Be a minimum of 25 years of age at the time of rental (21 for standard sedans with young driver fee)
  • Hold a valid driver's licence held for a minimum of 3 years
  • Present a valid government-issued photo ID and passport for international rentals
  • Provide a valid credit or debit card in the primary renter's name for the security deposit hold
  • Have no more than 2 driving offences in the preceding 36 months
⚠️ Renters of Executive Class and Sports category vehicles must be at least 28 years of age and hold a clean driving record for the past 5 years.

2. Booking & Confirmation

Reservations made via WhatsApp, email or our website are considered provisional until a written confirmation is sent by a PrestigeDrive concierge agent. A deposit of 25% of the total rental value is required to confirm bookings made more than 48 hours in advance.

3. Vehicle Use Restrictions

The following are strictly prohibited during the rental period:

  • Use in any organised motor sport, race, rally or off-road event
  • Transportation of more passengers than the vehicle's stated capacity
  • Driving under the influence of alcohol, drugs or any impairing substances
  • Sub-letting or re-renting the vehicle to any third party
  • Cross-border travel without prior written approval from PrestigeDrive
  • Use for commercial transport or rideshare services
  • Smoking, vaping or transporting pets without prior approval

4. Insurance & Liability

All PrestigeDrive vehicles are covered by comprehensive third-party liability insurance. The renter is responsible for the excess amount stated in the rental agreement in the event of any at-fault collision or damage. Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) is included in all Premium and Executive tier rentals.

5. Fuel Policy

All vehicles are provided with a full tank. Renters must return the vehicle with a full tank. If returned with less fuel, a refuelling charge will be applied at 1.5× the prevailing pump price plus a $20 administration fee.

6. Late Returns

A grace period of 60 minutes is allowed. After this, an additional day's rate will be charged. Renters wishing to extend must contact their concierge agent at least 24 hours before the agreed return time, subject to availability.

7. Damage Assessment

A full inspection is conducted at both collection and return. Any damage not noted at collection will be assessed against the renter's security deposit using independent assessments from certified prestige vehicle technicians.

8. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which the vehicle was rented. Disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of that territory.

Data We Collect

PrestigeDrive collects personal information necessary to process and manage bookings: full name, date of birth, contact details, driver's licence details, payment information and rental history.

How We Use Your Data

  • To confirm and manage your rental booking
  • To process payments and security deposits
  • To send booking confirmations via WhatsApp or email
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To improve our services and customer experience

Data Sharing

PrestigeDrive does not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be shared with payment processors, insurance underwriters, law enforcement (when legally required) and affiliated partner locations.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting concierge@prestigedrive.com. We will respond within 14 days.

🔒 PrestigeDrive uses industry-standard encryption and secure storage. We will never ask for passwords or full card details via WhatsApp or email.

Standard Cancellation Policy

  • 72+ hours before pickup: Full refund, no cancellation fee
  • 48–72 hours before pickup: 75% refund
  • 24–48 hours before pickup: 50% refund
  • Less than 24 hours: No refund
  • No-show: Full charge applies

Executive & Sports Class

  • Full refund if cancelled more than 7 days before pickup
  • 50% refund if cancelled 3–7 days before pickup
  • No refund within 72 hours of pickup

How to Cancel

All cancellations must be submitted in writing via WhatsApp or email to your concierge agent. Your confirmation number must be included in all cancellation requests. Verbal cancellations are not accepted.

✅ Our concierge team is always willing to discuss exceptional circumstances. If you have an emergency, contact us directly via WhatsApp.
