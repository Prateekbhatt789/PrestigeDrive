FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often. Still need help? Chat with us on WhatsApp.

You'll need a valid driver's licence held for a minimum of 3 years, a government-issued photo ID (passport for international renters), and a credit or debit card in the primary renter's name for the security deposit hold. All documents are verified at pickup.
All vehicles are provided with a full tank of fuel. We operate a full-to-full policy — simply return the car with a full tank and there are no fuel charges. If you'd prefer not to worry about it, you can add our prepaid Fuel Package at the time of booking.
Absolutely. Contact your concierge agent via WhatsApp at least 6 hours before your scheduled return and we'll arrange an extension subject to vehicle availability. Extensions are typically confirmed within the hour. Day-rate pricing applies to any additional days.
Yes — airport delivery and collection is included across all 32 cities we operate in. Our agent meets you at the arrivals hall, assists with luggage, and has the vehicle ready. Just share your flight details at booking and we handle the rest.
Cancellations made 72+ hours before pickup receive a full refund. Between 24–72 hours: 50% refund. Less than 24 hours: no refund. Executive and Sports class vehicles have a 7-day advance cancellation requirement for a full refund. We're always willing to discuss exceptional circumstances — just reach out.
A security deposit hold is placed on your credit card at pickup — the amount varies by vehicle class ($500–$5,000). This is a hold, not a charge, and is released within 3–5 business days of a clean vehicle return. The hold covers potential damage not covered by the CDW, as well as fuel and late fees if applicable.
